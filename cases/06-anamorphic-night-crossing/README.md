## Make it your own

- **Keep one dominant light direction and let everything obey it.** The image works because warm light comes from the left, the cyclist is rimmed from behind, and every reflection on the road stretches toward the camera. Adding a second warm source on the right flattens it into a snapshot.
- **The flare is a composition tool, not an effect.** One streak, sourced from a light that is actually in frame, at eye level. More than one and it reads as a filter.
- **Change the city by changing what is on the walls.** A Japanese back street, a Lisbon alley and a Chicago block differ in what glows, what is shuttered, what is stacked outside and how high the wires run. Swapping the noun without swapping the street furniture gives you the same picture with a different sign.
