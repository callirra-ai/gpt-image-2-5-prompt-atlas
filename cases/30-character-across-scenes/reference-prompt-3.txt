An empty environment photograph at dawn, 2:3 vertical, of a misty clearing in a pine forest, with a low wooden
footbridge. No people in the frame.

THE PLACE
A clearing in a mature pine forest: tall straight trunks receding into fog on both sides, a bare needle floor
with a few fallen branches, and patches of low ferns at the base of the nearest trees. A shallow stream crosses
the clearing from the left rear to the right foreground, running under a simple footbridge: three weathered
timber planks on two low stone abutments, with a single handrail on the near side. The far side of the clearing
opens into more fog, with the faintoutline of a second stand of trees.

LIGHT — the point of the reference
Dawn, twenty minutes after sunrise. One source only: a low sun outside the frame to the upper left, raking
almost horizontally through the trees. It is filtered through fog, so the light is soft, diffuse and volumetric
— visible as a broad shallow band of pale light occupying the middle distance, not as hard shafts. Everything
near the light band is warm and bright; everything closer to the camera and further away is cool blue-grey. The
fog is thickest at about eight metres' depth and thins toward the camera, so depth in the image is read almost
entirely from contrast rather than from detail. Long soft shadows run from the trunks toward the lower right.

COLOUR AND MATERIAL
Cool blue-grey ambient fog against warm pale gold where the light band sits. Wet bark is nearly black; the
needle floor is a muted red-brown; the bridge planks are weathered grey-brown with visible grain and a damp
sheen. Colour is muted overall, with low saturation and gentle contrast.

CONSTRAINTS
No people, no animals, no text, no signage, no vehicles, no litter, no campfire, no tent. No hard sun shafts
and no god rays — the light is diffuse in fog. Do not add colour accents; keep the palette blue-grey and pale
gold. Do not make the fog a flat white overlay; it must thin and thicken with depth.
