# RAW iPhone Quality - Subway Station

![An elderly couple on a US subway platform as a train blurs past behind them](preview.jpg)

A deliberately unprocessed phone snapshot: no grading, no depth of field, a crooked horizon and mixed fluorescent light. Use it wherever you need a photograph that reads as *found* rather than *taken* — documentary work, social campaigns, and any brief where a perfect image would break the story.

## Try the prompt

```text
Create a completely RAW quality, unprocessed, unedited image with full iPhone camera quality. A subway station in USA, a momentary blur. The subway is in motion. In front of the subway, there is an elderly woman and man.
```

[Copy plain text](prompt.txt) · [Full-size image](full.jpg)

## Make it your own

- **The whole look is one sentence.** `RAW quality, unprocessed, unedited image with full iPhone camera quality` does the heavy lifting. Swap the location freely; keep the sentence.
- **Ask for a fault.** A momentary blur, a crooked frame, visible noise. Remove the faults and the image immediately reverts to looking professional.
- **Do not add a subject action.** The couple is only standing and waiting. Give them a story and the snapshot quality evaporates.
