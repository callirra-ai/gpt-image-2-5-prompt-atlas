# 100 Pixel Art Items Grid

![A ten-by-ten grid of one hundred distinct pixel art objects, each labelled](preview.jpg)

A hundred unique pixel-art objects on a ten-by-ten grid, each with its own label. Use it for icon-set concepts, inventory screens, sticker sheets and any brief that is really a counting and layout test.

## Try the prompt

```text
Create a single image containing a grid of 100 completely unique pixel art items, each with a meaningful label
```

[Copy plain text](prompt.txt) · [Full-size image](full.jpg)

## Make it your own

- **Change the theme, not the count.** Potions, tools, fruit, spaceships. The number is what makes it impressive; the subject is what makes it usable.
- **Insist on `no repeats` explicitly.** A grid of mostly-unique items with three duplicates is a normal model outcome. The phrase is the whole contract.
- **Keep the labels short.** One or two words per item. Longer labels start competing with the art at thumbnail size.

## Settings and result

`openai/gpt-image-2.5-sunburst` · quality `xhigh` · 1:1 · **3122 image tokens** · 63 s · $0.0938

A hundred cells, a hundred objects, and the grid holds. Density is what makes this card work — it is the kind of image that stops a scroll because you keep checking whether the items really are all different.

The labels are present and legible in the full-size file. They are not meant to be read at thumbnail size, and at that scale the grid becomes a texture — which is a second useful way to use it.
