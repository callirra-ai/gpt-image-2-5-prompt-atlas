# Pet Brand Collaboration Poster

![A white cat in a KFC apron serving a bucket of fried chicken, with Chinese promotion text](preview.jpg)

A pet-brand collaboration poster: a cat in a uniform behind a counter, a full promotion kit, and shelf after shelf of invented packaging. Use it for pet-brand concepts, seasonal campaign mockups, and any brief about taking a brand's visual language somewhere absurd.

## Try the prompt

```text
Theme: "77 (cat's name) X KFC" collaboration poster. Generate a collaboration poster featuring the same pet (absolutely consistent in appearance and coloring) with KFC brand identity (red-and-white color scheme, classic logo, restaurant scenes, etc.). Have the cat wear a KFC employee uniform, KFC employee hat, and name badge while standing at the counter, selling fried chicken, burgers, and combo meals, and interacting with chicken buckets, fries, soda, and other brand elements. The style should be lively and fun with a commercial co-branding feel, suitable for online promotion and event posters. Add appropriate Chinese copy freely for the poster.
```

[Copy plain text](prompt.txt) · [Full-size image](full.jpg)

## Make it your own

- **Give the pet a job, not a costume.** A uniform and a counter position read as a brand campaign; a hat alone reads as a stock photo.
- **Ask for the whole campaign kit.** Menu board, cups, bags, a promotion badge. One poster looks like a poster; a kit looks like a brand.
- **Do not name a real brand if you plan to publish it.** Describe the visual language — red and white, a fried chicken counter — and let the model build a lookalike you can actually ship.
