A keynote photographed from inside the crowd: a stage in the distance, a giant product screen, and a foreground of heads and raised phones. Use it for event coverage, product-launch illustration, and any brief about being one of many people in a room.

## Try the prompt

```text
Amateur iPhone photo at Apple Park during the iPhone 20 keynote, Tim Cook presenting on stage. Shot from the crowd at a distance
```

[Copy plain text](prompt.txt) · [Full-size image](full.jpg)

## Make it your own

- **Keep the crowd in the foreground.** That is the entire frame. Remove it and you have a stage photograph, which is a different and much more common image.
- **Say `amateur` and `from the crowd`.** Both phrases are load-bearing. Without them the model produces a polished press shot.
- **Fill the screen with a real product.** A slide with an invented product name still reads as a keynote.
