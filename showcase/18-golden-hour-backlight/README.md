## Try the prompt

```text
85mm portrait photograph backlit at golden hour with the sun directly behind the subject's head, hair glowing as a bright halo, face in soft open shade with bounced fill only, strong natural lens flare streaking across the frame, dust in the air catching the light, long shadows running toward the camera, real skin texture with visible pores and fine lines, no airbrushing, no plastic skin, no digital over-sharpening, no watermark, no text
```

[Copy plain text](prompt.txt) · [Full-size Flare](full-flare.jpg) · [Full-size Sunburst](full-sunburst.jpg)

## Make it your own

- **Put the sun in the frame.** Directly behind the head. The flare and the halo are the picture.
- **Leave the face in shade.** No fill flash. The soft, slightly dark face against the bright rim is what makes this look expensive.
- **Ask for dust.** Airborne particles catching the light are what turn a backlit portrait into a golden-hour portrait.

## Settings and result

`openai/gpt-image-2.5-flare` and `openai/gpt-image-2.5-sunburst` · quality `xhigh` · 3:4 · **2459 image tokens** · 24 s

Rendered on both tiers. **Flare is the better of the two here** — the flare streaks across the frame and the halo reads as a real rim, where Sunburst gives a slightly flatter, more even result. This is the clearest separation between the tiers in the comparison set.

Skin texture survives the backlight, which is the usual failure point: backlit face renders commonly come back plastic.
